How to Join

Sign up for a class

Class sizes are intentionally limited. Browse the schedule and reserve your spot in advance.

Complete the waiver

Required before your first class. Takes 2 minutes - you can complete it in advance or on the day of class.

Complete Waiver

Donate

We operate on a pay-what-you-can basis. Donations cover the cost of renting the studio, maintaining the website, and compensating the teacher. You're welcome to give now, when you arrive, or after class, whatever works for you. Give more if you're able, less if you need.

Attend the class

Arrive 10 minutes early - the door locks 2 minutes after class begins.

Bring your mat and water bottle. Blocks are optional, but recommended. Take shoes off before entering studio.

Be respectful to self and others to cultivate an inclusive and supportive space.

All levels welcome.
