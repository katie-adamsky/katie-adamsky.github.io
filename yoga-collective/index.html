Frequently asked questions

When is it?
Classes run 10–11 AM every Sunday from May 3 through August 30, 2026. No class on May 10, May 24, or July 5. Doors open 10 minutes before class starts.
Where is it?
Whim W'him Contemporary Dance Center, 1716 2nd Ave N, Seattle, WA 98109 (Queen Anne). Get directions →
Who is teaching?
We are a collective of newly graduated teachers from Synergy Yoga Teacher Training, excited to share what we've learned. Specific instructor details are revealed when you sign up for individual classes.
What should I bring?
Bring your own mat and water bottle. Blankets, straps, and blocks are optional. Layered clothing is recommended as the studio temperature varies.
How can I respect the space?
Arrive 10 minutes early — the door locks 2 minutes after class begins. Remove shoes at the entrance. Silence your phone and fitness watch. During class, listen to your body and rest as needed.
What is parking like?
Pretty easy. There's a parking lot next to the adjacent church, plus additional street parking nearby.
How much does it cost?
Pay what you can — give more if you're able, less if you need. Suggested donation is $10–$25. We accept Venmo (@cityyogacollective) and Zelle (Monique Thakkar | cityyogacollective@gmail.com).
What level and style of yoga?
Classes vary by teacher and may be Hatha, Vinyasa, or Yin. Some may include philosophy, chanting, breathwork, and meditation. All levels are welcome.
Is there an age limit?
Classes are best suited for those 16 and older.
How do you honor the roots of yoga?
We are committed to continuing our education, practicing with integrity, and sharing yoga in a way that respects its South Asian origins while making it accessible to our community.
